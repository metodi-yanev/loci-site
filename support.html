Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Loci opens straight to your decks. There's nothing to sign up for.

Does Loci work offline?

Yes, completely. Loci never connects to the internet, so it works the same on a plane as at home.

How do I add cards from a list or a spreadsheet?

On the home screen, tap Paste cards to paste text, or Import a file to pick one. Put one card per row, with the front in the first column and the back in the second.

  • CSV, TSV and TXT files work, saved as UTF-8 text.
  • A tab, comma or semicolon between the columns is detected for you.
  • Only the first two columns are imported.
  • If the first row has column names, turn on “First row is a header”.
  • Images aren't imported, only text.

Most flashcard apps and spreadsheets can export to CSV or plain text. In Loci, tap What files work? on the import screen for step-by-step instructions.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

In Settings, tap Back up now. Loci saves all your decks, cards, images and progress to one .loci file, which you can keep anywhere: your files, a cloud drive, or a computer. On the new phone, install Loci, open Settings and tap Restore from backup.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) usually includes Loci as well, but a .loci file is the surest copy.

How do I take my cards to another app?

Open a deck, tap its ⋯ menu and choose Export cards. You get a text file with the front and back of every card, which other flashcard apps and spreadsheets can open.

My daily reminder didn't show up

  • Check that notifications for Loci are allowed in your device's settings.
  • The reminder only comes on days you haven't studied yet, and only when cards are due.
  • A study day runs until 4 a.m. If your reminder is set after midnight and you studied that evening, it's skipped, because that night still counts as the same day.
  • It doesn't show while Loci is open, or when Focus or Do Not Disturb is on.
  • On Android, battery saving can delay a reminder by a few minutes.

How does Loci decide when I see a card again?

Loci uses FSRS, a modern spaced repetition method. Each time you rate a card, it estimates when you're about to forget it and brings it back just before then. Cards you know well come back less and less often.

Is Loci really free?

Yes. No ads, no limits, no subscription. See the pledge.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a deck in the app to remove it and its cards (your streak and statistics stay), or uninstall Loci to delete everything. Loci keeps no copy anywhere else; see the privacy policy.
