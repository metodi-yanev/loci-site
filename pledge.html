The Loci Pledge

Flashcard apps tend to start free and then close in: sign-up walls, ads, daily limits, features moved behind a subscription. Loci: Flashcards & Study won't. These are its promises.

  • Free forever Everything Loci does today stays free. No trial, no subscription, no limit on decks, cards or study sessions.
  • No ads, no interruptions No ads, no upsells, no pop-ups you didn't ask for, no prompts to rate the app in the middle of studying.
  • No account Loci opens straight to your cards. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.
  • Your cards stay yours Everything stays on your device. Loci collects no data and doesn't connect to the internet. You can export your cards or back up everything at any time, so you're never locked in.

If paid extras ever exist

The core app is free forever. If optional paid extras are ever added, they'll be announced before they ship, and they will never take away or limit anything that's free.
