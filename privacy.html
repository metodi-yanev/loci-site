Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy covers Loci: Flashcards & Study (“Loci”), the flashcard app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

  • Loci collects no data. Not your cards, not how you use the app, not who you are.
  • There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Loci doesn't connect to the internet. Everything you make stays on your device.

Who this policy is from

Loci is made by Metodi Yanev, an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy, write to metodi.yanev@proton.me.

What stays on your device

Loci stores your decks, cards, the images you add to cards, your study history and progress, and your settings. All of it lives only in the app's storage on your device. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else, and I have no way to see it.

What Loci collects

Nothing. Loci has no servers and no account to sign in to. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from me or from any third party.

Permissions

Loci asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn each one off in your device's settings.

  • Camera: only when you take a photo to add to a card. The photo is saved in Loci, on your device.
  • Photos: only when you add an image to a card. Loci receives just the image you pick.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder in Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from a server.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export a deck, Loci writes a file and hands it to your device's save or share screen. You choose where it goes. Once you save it to a cloud drive, send it by email or share it with someone, that service's own privacy policy applies. Backup files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Your device's own backup (iCloud on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) may include Loci's data if you have it turned on. That backup belongs to your Apple or Google account, under their privacy policies; I can't access it.

The App Store and Google Play

Apple and Google run the stores you download Loci from. They may share anonymous, aggregated figures with developers, such as install counts, and crash reports if you've chosen to share them. These don't identify you to me. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover what they collect.

Children

Loci is meant for people aged 13 and over. It collects no information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Delete a deck in the app to remove it, its cards and its images. Your study statistics (streak and daily totals) are kept until you uninstall Loci, which deletes everything it stored on your device. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you the right to access, correct or delete the personal data held about you. Because I hold none, there is nothing to access or delete, but you're welcome to write and ask.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Loci ever offers something that needs your data, such as optional sync, it will be opt-in, and this policy will be updated before it ships.
